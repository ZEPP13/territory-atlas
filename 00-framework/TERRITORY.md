# Official Sales Territory — Joe Nelson, Emerson Flow (as issued)

to county-border down the middle of the state. Rappahannock and Mecklenburg are the westernmost
edge cases and both fall INSIDE.

This supersedes the "east of US-29" working assumption from Framework v0.2. Net changes:
- **ADDED**: Culpeper, Madison, Greene, Orange, Rappahannock (were boundary band or out)
- **REMOVED**: Halifax, Charlotte, Prince Edward, Appomattox, Campbell, Lynchburg, Danville,
  Pittsylvania, Nelson, Amherst (were in C9/C11 or boundary band)
- Albemarle, Charlottesville, Fluvanna, Louisa, Fauquier are confirmed **IN** (no longer "band")
